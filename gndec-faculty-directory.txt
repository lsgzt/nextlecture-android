HOW MUCH IS ACTUALLY PUBLISHED

  A phone number is published for 222 of them (171 mobile,
  186 office line or intercom): the GNDEC Telephone Directory, the
  Information Brochure 2026 and the 2026 permanent-section lists carry one.
  The web directory itself still publishes no per-faculty phone number.

  Anything not shown below simply has no published number - it is NOT an omission
  in this compilation. No number here has been guessed, derived from an email
  address, or taken from a third-party site.

CONFIDENCE
  'High'    - Information Brochure 2026, the college's own current directory, and
               the 2026 permanent-section lists (the mentor's own number).
  'Medium'  - GNDEC Telephone Directory and the academic-council / examination
               rosters (2023-26 tenure). These are older; the holder may have
               changed role or left. Marked in the entry.
  Where the two disagree, both numbers are shown rather than one being chosen.

directory now carries the other published spellings of a teacher under
"also_published_as", so a timetable label such as "DR APK SODHI" or
"ER. JASWANT SINGH TAUR" opens the right person instead of a second, empty
record. The list is in the entry itself.

  college's intercom where the Telephone Directory gives one.
